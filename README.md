# 🍽️ Restaurant Management System

A web-based **Restaurant Management System** designed to simplify and manage the day-to-day operations of a restaurant.

The system provides a centralized dashboard for managing orders, menu items, customers, inventory, sales, expenses, and business analytics.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Managing restaurant operations manually can be time-consuming and error-prone. This project aims to provide a simple and efficient digital solution for restaurant management.

The system allows restaurant staff/administrators to monitor important business activities from a single dashboard and make data-driven decisions using sales and performance analytics.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Dashboard
- Overview of restaurant activities
- Total orders
- Total sales/revenue
- Profit overview
- Available dishes
- Recent orders
- Customer statistics

### 🍴 Menu Management
- Add food items
- Update food details
- Delete food items
- Manage food categories
- Manage item pricing and availability

### 🧾 Order Management
- View orders
- Track order status
- Manage completed and pending orders
- View recent orders

### 📦 Inventory Management
- Track available stock
- Monitor inventory levels
- Identify low-stock items
- Manage stock information

### 👥 Customer Management
- Store customer information
- View customer details
- Track customer activity

### 💰 Sales & Expense Management
- Track daily sales
- Record expenses
- Calculate revenue
- Monitor profit/loss

### 📈 Analytics & Reports
- Revenue analysis
- Sales trends
- Best-selling food items
- Customer statistics
- Generate useful reports
